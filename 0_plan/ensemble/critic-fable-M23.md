**7. The decision to draw the whole diagram in HTML boxes with no SVG. The right call, for a better reason than the one given, and with one cost it did not price.**

Right, and not marginally. The M10 defect the rationale cites — labels declared at 14px rendering at 11 to 13px because SVG text scales with the viewport transform and no `calc()` inverts it — is a symptom of the general rule, which is that a diagram whose nodes contain flowing body copy and a live interactive control must be laid out by the text engine, not by a `viewBox`. This map's nodes carry 101- and 130-character sentences from a fixed copy deck that must wrap correctly at nineteen widths; SVG `<text>` does not wrap at all, and `<foreignObject>` is the only alternative and brings its own support and accessibility problems. The terminal nodes contain real `<a>` elements with 44px targets, real `:focus-visible` rings and real hover states, all of which would have needed ARIA scaffolding and none of which would have kept its geometry under a scaling transform.

The decisive evidence is not M10 at all, and the rationale does not use it: **`V2-rootx2.jpg` and `V18.jpg`**. At a 32px root the page reflows with no horizontal scroll, and at 320×256 the same. An SVG map with a fixed `viewBox` would not respond to the root font at any zoom level — the type around it would double and the diagram would stay exactly where it was, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 problem the HTML build simply does not have. `forced-colors.jpg` is the second piece: every segment, dot, hairline and strip rule is redrawn in `CanvasText` by one block at the end of the sheet, inherited by the diagram because the diagram is made of the same boxes as the rest of the page.

The cost, and it is a real one, is that connector endpoints become arithmetic on percentages rather than references to the boxes they point at. `w-down` is solved as `calc((100% - var(--m23-rgap)) / 2 - var(--m23-attach))`, which asserts that the two door blocks are equal halves of the map. CSS cannot check that assertion, and at V13 it is false by 25.8px, so the arm that should land on "GT Anywhere" lands 13px above it (my probe; fix 4). In SVG you would compute both endpoints from the nodes' own boxes and the error could not occur. The second unpriced cost is that the diagram has no machine-readable topology: nothing in the DOM says ADMISSIONS connects to THE PROGRAM, and the connectors are `aria-hidden` `<i>` elements. That is acceptable here, but only because every sentence inside the map is also present in the comparison table below, so a non-visual reader loses the picture and no facts — and the rationale should say that out loud rather than leave it to a critic to notice. The third is that DD-21 is only half drawn: the phone spine genuinely takes a third, fourth or fifth stub without a redraw, and the 1024-and-above split does not, because every new terminal needs its own `--m23-attach` constant and a re-solve of the halving.

Net: keep the HTML. Anchor the wire's grid span to the cards so the geometry stops asserting what the layout does not guarantee, add the two door **block** heights to the measurement harness so a false premise fails the runner instead of a critic, and record in the rationale the two arguments that actually carry the decision — text resize and forced colours — alongside the M10 one.
