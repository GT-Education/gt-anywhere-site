## E. Recommendation

**M1 best serves L1.** Its equal, fully clickable doors and prominent filled Explore treatments make the next action easiest to recognize. Its main routing-layout correction is small: bring the comparison entry fully inside the mobile first screen and preserve adequate target sizes.

**M2 best combines the mission and exclusivity lenses.** The admission bar, academic register and restrained hierarchy communicate earned selectivity. M3 conveys quiet prestige but contributes less intellectual substance; M4’s current crop and activity strip do not yet establish the stronger student-work argument its direction promises.

**Recommend M1 to Nari as the base direction, subject to the shared fixes above.** The runner-ups are **M5**, which would need one link per door, a trustworthy schedule graphic and clearer desktop composition; and **M2**, which would need its mobile cues restored and its register/first-screen comparison reconciled with the routing hierarchy. M5’s combined format/name labels and M2’s academic structure are useful elements to test within the chosen direction.
