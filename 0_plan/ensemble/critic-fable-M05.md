### B. The button corner: the correction, and the reasoning for DD-47's uniform shape

card's shape, not a button's**: reproducing it on an `<a>` means two pseudo-elements or a `clip-path` on eight
controls, and then re-proving the focus ring (which follows the border box, not a clip), the 44px hit area and
forced colours on each. The rationale costs that out honestly rather than waving at it. Third, **DD-47 exists so

decision to record it as such (`RATIONALE.md:254`).

One caveat the engineer should carry forward: the argument's force comes from the estate's *application* control,
and this page has no application control on its first screen — its two CTAs route to school sites. That does not
weaken the choice, but it means the claim is "the estate's apply-button shape on a routing button", not "the same
