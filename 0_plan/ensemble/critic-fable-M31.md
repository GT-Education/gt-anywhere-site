How the elision reads, which is the judgement asked for: **as damage more than as editing.** "We answered that question in Georgetown, Texas, where a flagship K–8 campus was launched […]." is the school speaking in its own first person. So: keep it while the escalation is open, and settle it before the mock goes to Nari. The clean resolution is the rationale's own third option — end the sentence at "was launched." on the page, a complete and true sentence, and keep the withholding recorded in Flagged slots where every other withholding already lives.
