Two scope gaps to name, neither of which currently hides a defect:

- **Mark against mark is unmeasured.** Everything in the inventory is compared to `runs`, which are
  glyph rects. Nothing compares the registration cross to a corner handle, or a handle to a drawn
  rule. The engineer's "clearance is over 700px at the four phone rows" is a one-off probe, not a
  harness row, and I reproduced it (724.25 at V2 against their 724.3) — but the guard for that defect
  is construction, not the runner.
- **Pseudo-element type is not in the ink inventory.** `walk()` collects text nodes only, so the
  stacked table's 18 `.compare td::before` labels at every phone row and the four `.steps li::before`
  numerals at every row are not "glyphs" to the harness, even though a reader sees them. I checked
  their neighbours by hand: the nearest lines are 16px away and nothing is at risk today. But a
  harness that fixed an incomplete *line* inventory still has an incomplete *ink* inventory, and the
  symmetry is worth writing down.

Everything else about the extension is sound. Rebuilding the pseudo-element drop from resolved insets
is the right technique and it works; measuring against canvas ink with a `Math.abs(ink.box - rect.height) <= 1.5`
guard before trusting the inset is careful; excluding clipped 1px boxes is correct; modelling the
L-shaped handle per painted arm rather than as its union is correct and is the difference between 32
phantom crossings and none.
