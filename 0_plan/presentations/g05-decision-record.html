Six positioning decisions for the new gt.school landing

The umbrella page that routes a parent to the Georgetown campus or to GT Anywhere. These six decisions govern every mock, headline and ad that follows.

The decisions 6 of 6 decided

01
Umbrella sentence
DD-33DD-20REQ-021
What it was

Neither site tells a parent there are two ways to attend, or what GT stands for.

Decision

"GT School is a selective school for gifted and talented children, in person at our Georgetown, Texas campus or online from anywhere."

Why

It answers the two questions every first-time parent has before they can choose a school.

02
Speed figure
Q-019CR-004 to 008REQ-022
What it was

In July the Anywhere homepage carried 3x, 4x and 3-7x at once. Today only 3x remains, on both sites and in the refund policy.

Decision

No speed figure on the landing. 3X is the only candidate, and only after the data behind it is published.

Why

The sites now agree on 3X and the refund policy guarantees it, but no data has been published, and the press is asking the network exactly that question.

03
Accreditation
Q-033CR-001 to 003REQ-004
What it was

Three versions are live today: "fully accredited by Cognia", "Candidate for Accreditation", and plain "Accredited" in gt.school's footer and Anywhere's structured data.

Decision

No accreditation claim and no Cognia mark on any new surface until the true status is confirmed with counsel. The live sites are corrected to one true wording.

Why

Only one version can be true, and "Candidate" means not yet accredited. A wrong claim here is a legal problem, not a copy problem.

04
Georgetown tuition
Q-011CR-024MD-25
What it was

No price on any Georgetown page, only a $10,000 scholarship banner with nothing to subtract from. Anywhere states $10,400 a year.

Decision

"Tuition on request" on the landing until the figure is supplied, then published the way Anywhere publishes its own.

Why

Parents cannot compare two schools when one has a price and the other does not. The figure cannot be invented.

05
Title for the adults
Q-035DD-27REQ-076
What it was

gt.school says Guides, Anywhere says Advisors, and some are the same people with identical bios.

Decision

Guides, everywhere. Anywhere renames its Advisors copy, page, structured data and Terms of Use.

Recommendation was Advisors. Johnny chose Guides.
Why

One staff body needs one name, or parents assume two roles. Guides is the word gt.school already uses 47 times and matches the network.

06
Alpha School relationship
MD-17MD-16REQ-021
What it was

Anywhere calls itself "The Gifted Academy of Alpha School". gt.school does not mention Alpha.

Decision

One small line, once: "The gifted school in the Alpha School network."

Why

The link is already public on both sides, so hiding it looks evasive. One line keeps GT's own results separate from press questions about the network.
