# M21 The Specimen

Shared rules: `CONTRACT.md` v2.1 (fold method with computed h1 lines, the compact door rule on phone rows, prose at 1.5, K3's two-colour ring, crop-aware image widths, DD-47's uniform button corner).

**Borrowing rule, binding on this brief and on M22 to M25.** These five directions draw their composition from the Design Library at `~/projects/libraries/design`, which holds nine reverse-designed languages with measured values. Borrow the composition, the rhythm and the structural devices. Never the palette and never the typefaces. GT School's ground, surfaces, navy, accent and the three faces Literata, Inter Tight and Inconsolata are fixed by K12's "what never changes" list (G1, G3, G12) and by `tokens-v2.css`. A mock that imports a source palette or face is off-brief, not a variation (DD-48).
