# M24 One at a Time

Shared rules: `CONTRACT.md` v2.1. The borrowing rule at the head of `M21.md` binds this brief (DD-48).

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Mock id and name | M24, "One at a Time". Lens order L3 mission, L4 exclusivity, L1 objective, L2 continuity. New direction, from the Design Library |
| Source language | `insp-palebeat`, "Pale Beat", primary: one ground that never changes, holding one centred object at a time in negative space that occupies roughly three quarters of the frame, with type kept small and set well below the object, no headline competing with it, and exactly one accent per beat, never two at once. Sections stack so the page reads as a sequence of beats rather than a layout. `insp-outship`, support: long still holds and one italic emphasis word per sentence |
| What is deliberately not borrowed | The source's pale lilac canvas and its per-section accent hue changes, which would give a school four unrelated colours; and its complete absence of a headline, which the contract forbids, because the fixed sentence and both calls to action are required on the first screen at every row. The restraint therefore begins at the second screen, and the rationale must say so plainly rather than claim a purity the page does not have |
| Thesis | A school confident enough to say one thing per screen. After the choice, every screen carries a single drawn object and a single line beneath it, so a parent reads the argument as a sequence of statements instead of a wall. The objects are drawn from the school's own facts, not decoration |
| Exemplars | The library's `examples/stillpoint.html` for the beat sequence. Within this repository, `exemplars/barnes-foundation-1440-full.jpg` for one saturated colour spent once (C03) and `exemplars/exeter-1440-full.jpg` for a figure that carries a label and a link (C04). Avoided: `exemplars/every-1440-full.jpg`'s dense directory, which is the opposite of this direction (C26) |
| Composition | First screen as the contract requires: header 56, h1 at 8 columns, provenance, two doors and the compare anchor, all present at every row. From the second screen the page becomes beats. Each beat is a full-width section on `--color-bg` holding one centred object of at most 320px square, with at least 96px of clear space above and below it at 1024 and above and 64px on phones, and beneath the object one line of type: a `--font-mono` `--text-xs` uppercase tracked label, then the deck sentence it belongs to at `--text-base`, centred, at most 45 characters a line. Four beats in order: the admission bar, the program, the two places, the year. Then the comparison table, which is the one dense object on the page and is allowed to be, because a table is what a parent asked for |
| Typography | Contract roles. The beat sentences are the deck's own; one word in each is set in Literata italic, chosen so it never falls at a line end |
| Color and surface | One ground, `--color-bg`, for every beat: the page never changes ground until the navy footer. Objects are drawn in `--color-navy` hairlines at 1.5px. Exactly one accent per beat and never two at once: the admission beat spends it on a single filled arc segment, the places beat on one filled dot, and the other two beats spend none. Focus per contract |
| Imagery | Diagrammatic; no photograph in either state. Objects are original monoline geometry built from the school's facts: a segmented arc whose filled portion is the admission bar, two concentric rings for the two places, a four-mark row for the intensive weekends, a single tile grid for the program. No object may imply a figure the deck does not carry, and no object may be a chart with an unlabelled axis |
| Motion | Still, and this direction is where stillness is the argument rather than a limitation. V1 and V3 instant; V10 under `no-preference`. Reduced motion: `--duration-none`. The source's snap between beats is a film device and is not borrowed |
| Copy | Sections 0 to 8 verbatim; beat labels from section 9. No new words and no figure the deck does not carry |
| Conversion | Doors on the first screen at every row; compare anchor under them; the four beats; the table; proof; steps; secondary CTAs; footer |
| Acceptance | Both critics at or above 4, Mission and Motion expected at 5. Matrix as the contract. Exactly one object and one line per beat, verified in the captures. No beat carries two accent uses, measured. Every object must survive forced colours, checked in a capture. Rationale names DD-38, DD-39, DD-47, DD-48 |
