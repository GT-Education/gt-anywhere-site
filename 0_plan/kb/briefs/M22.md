# M22 Hard Cut

Shared rules: `CONTRACT.md` v2.1. The borrowing rule at the head of `M21.md` binds this brief: borrow composition, rhythm and structural devices, never the palette and never the typefaces (DD-48).

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Mock id and name | M22, "Hard Cut". Lens order L1 objective, L3 mission, L2 continuity, L4 exclusivity. New direction, from the Design Library |
| Source language | `insp-outship`, "Cream Hard-Cut Editorial", primary: a warm editorial page cut entirely on hard cuts with zero transitions and long still holds, complete sentences centred on the canvas, and exactly one italic emphasis word per sentence. `insp-cloudstudio`, "Pastel Hard-Cut", support: a full-bleed ground that changes per chapter and never crossfades, with deliberately thin chrome and a small bracketed uppercase label in wide tracking opening each stage |
| What is deliberately not borrowed | The source's cream-ink-vermilion palette, which is close enough to GT's to be tempting and is still not ours; its glyph-particle fields; and Cloudstudio's dithered creature and driver pill bar, which are a mascot and a tour control that a school routing two real destinations has no use for. Name each omission in the rationale |
| Thesis | The page is a sequence of chapters, each one a full-bleed ground with one thing to say, cut hard against the next with no gradient and no shared edge. A parent moves through it the way a reader turns a page rather than the way a visitor scrolls a wall. The choice is the first chapter and everything after it is one idea at a time |
| Exemplars | The library's `examples/kilnworks.html` for the editorial cut rhythm and `examples/tideline.html` for the chapter swap. Within this repository, `exemplars/tate-1440.jpg` for two identically weighted calls to action (C01). Avoided: `exemplars/university-of-hull-1440-full.jpg`, where a second campus is buried in a filter chip (C20) |
| Composition | Every section is a full-bleed chapter with its own ground and its own vertical padding, and the boundary between two chapters is a hard edge with no rule, no gradient and no shadow. Chapter order and ground: 1 the choice on `--color-bg`; 2 the comparison on `--color-surface`; 3 the proof on `--color-anywhere-tint`; 4 the steps on `--color-navy`; 5 current families on `--color-bg`; footer navy. Each chapter opens with a bracketed uppercase label in `--font-mono` `--text-xs` tracked 0.08em, centred, then its content. First screen: header 56, h1 at 8 columns, provenance, the two doors 6 and 6, and the compare anchor, all left-aligned on the grid rather than centred, because the fixed sentence is 22 words and centring it costs a line at every phone row: state that departure from the source plainly |
| Typography | Contract roles. Exactly one word per fixed sentence is set in Literata italic for emphasis, chosen so it never falls at a line end: on the h1 the word is "or", the hinge of the choice. Chapter labels `--font-mono`; everything else as the contract |
| Color and surface | The four GT grounds above are the whole palette of the page and they carry the cut. One accent use per chapter: on the first screen a 3px `--color-accent` rule under the compare anchor; in the comparison chapter the accent is spent on nothing, which is allowed and should be said. On `--color-navy`, `--color-on-navy` text at 14.58 and `--color-text-muted-on-dark` for secondary. Focus per contract, two-colour ring on navy |
| Imagery | Default state: the school marks only, sized to K12 V2. Photographed state after RF-25 and DD-42: one 3:2 photograph filling the width of a single chapter, at most 720 CSS px, with the chapter's ground behind it; no photograph on the first screen |
| Motion | Still. The source's cut rhythm of 1.5 to 3 seconds is a film's and is borrowed as a spatial device only: the hard edge between chapters is the cut. No crossfade anywhere, which is a rule the stylesheet should state as a comment rather than a token. V10 native anchor scroll under `no-preference`. Reduced motion: `--duration-none` |
| Copy | Sections 0 to 8 verbatim. The chapter labels come from section 9; no new words |
| Conversion | Doors on the first screen at every row; compare anchor on the first screen at V14 and V16 and the second at V2 and V5; table with repeated values; proof; steps; secondary CTAs; current families; footer |
