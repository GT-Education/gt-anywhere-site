| Mock id and name | M33, "The Scale". Lens order L3 mission, L4 exclusivity, L1 objective, L2 continuity. New direction, from Johnny's reading of the animated story section on the live gt.school about page, 2026-09-11 |
| Where it comes from | The `story_wrap` section of `https://www.gt.school/about-us`, read as source text and stylesheet, never in a browser. It is the only animated storytelling device the estate has built, and Johnny asked for it recreated as a standalone page |
| The source mechanic, measured from its own stylesheet | A sticky stage, `position: sticky; top: 6rem; height: calc(100vh - 6rem)`, laid out as a two-column grid that becomes one column with four rows on small screens. Down one side, `timeline_lines-part-wrap` runs the full height as a column with `justify-content: space-between`, holding **155 tick marks**: each `timeline_small-line` is 0.9px tall and 30% wide, aligned right, with an inner bar at 50% width in the estate's light gold; **49 of the 155 carry `is-bigger`** and two carry `is-hour`. One rule sets `opacity: 0` on a tick, which is the animation: the marks arrive as the reader descends. A 1px full-height gold line, `timeline_line-wrap is-gold`, runs the scale's edge. The stage holds a column of three image frames at `aspect-ratio: 6/5` on a 15 per cent gold tint with a large radius, between short gold rules 2.75rem long and vertical hairlines 5rem tall that fade at both ends, and a text column with 4.5rem gaps |
| Thesis | A school whose whole argument is measurement tells its story against a measure. The scale is not decoration: it is the page's spine, the reader's position on it, and the reason the story reads as something that happened over time rather than a list of assertions |
| Motion, and the permission it needs | This is **V11 under MN-17**, a new vocabulary entry proposed the same day and still open: scroll-driven progress along a measured scale. Build it in **CSS only**, with `animation-timeline`, so no script is involved and D-039 does not gate it. The guards are the vocabulary's own: one use per page, never inside the first two screens, transform and opacity only. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` every tick is present at rest, every duration is `--duration-none`, and the page reads identically without a single scroll. In a browser without scroll-driven animation the scale is fully drawn and static, which is the same no-JavaScript state every other mock ships. State all of this in the Motion spec and say plainly that the move is proposed rather than approved |
| What is deliberately not taken | The estate's light gold for the ticks, which is its accent and not a token we spend 155 times; the three photographic frames, because every asset shows identifiable children and DD-42 and RF-25 apply; and the illustration behind the stage, which is a Webflow asset we do not own. Name each |
