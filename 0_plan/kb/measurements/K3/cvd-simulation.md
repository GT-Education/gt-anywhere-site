## Supplementary: deltaSRGB per pair, per simulated vision

Euclidean distance between the pair's two SIMULATED colors in 8-bit sRGB space (per channel 0-255; maximum possible distance is about 441.7). Not a WCAG measure and not a calibrated perceptual difference (sRGB is not perceptually uniform) - reported only to show whether the pair's colors move closer together or farther apart under each simulated vision, since contrast ratio alone is a luminance measure and these matrices hold luminance roughly constant by design.
