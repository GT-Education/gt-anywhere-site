GT School is the gifted academy of the Alpha School family. Alpha proved that students can master core academics in a few focused hours a day and love school while doing it. GT asked the next question: what happens when you apply that model to the students who are ready to go further and faster?

We answered that question in Georgetown, Texas, where a flagship K–8 campus was launched to prove that 3X learning velocity isn’t just a slogan, but a measurable fact. The pilot answered emphatically, with students achieving multiple grade levels of acceleration while deepening their academic interests, and all while loving school.

Since then, GT School has grown on every axis. GT Anywhere launched in 2026, bringing the full model to gifted families nationwide. This K-8 virtual academy attracted hundreds of students in its first months and continues to build momentum. The same philosophy extends to GT Summer Camps which have turned school vacations into the proving ground gifted kids actually want.
