The estate’s two-door panel, rebuilt and extended

This reproduces the two-door panel at the foot of gt.school/about-us, measured from the live component rather than redrawn by eye. Hover or tab to either side: the panel widens from 644 to 773 pixels against its sibling's 515, the navy blur fades in, the headline and paragraph open from zero to 216 pixels over 300 milliseconds, and the button turns from navy to accent orange. Every one of those figures was measured, not estimated.

Two things here are deliberately unlike our own mocks, because this is a copy of the live page. The photographs show identifiable children, which our mocks do not use pending written permissions. And the right-hand paragraph carries the learning-speed multiple, which our own decision keeps off any landing surface because the figure is unverified. Neither is a proposal; both are what the live page says today.

One thing is deliberately better. The original reveals on mouse hover only and cannot be reached from a keyboard. This responds to focus as well, and holds still under reduced motion.

Three changes on Johnny’s direction, which take this past a pure replica. The site header is rebuilt above it, measured the same way. Each panel now names its school, so the campus reads as GT Georgetown rather than only GT School. And each panel carries both actions rather than one, an outlined Learn more beside a filled Apply now. The estate has no precedent for two actions on one panel, so the pairing follows its own header instead; on a photograph the outline is white rather than the header’s warm grey, and that is the only value here not taken from the source. Both panels keep an identical construction, because the two doors must not be ranked.
