# Concept catalog, batch C: Tate, Yale School of Architecture, Harvard University, Crimson Global Academy, AREA 17, Equinox

Six of K0's twenty shortlisted exemplars (`K0-design-hunt.md` F8/F9), captured 2026-09-10/11 per `EXEMPLARS.md` Batch 2. Method: all 24 screenshots (four per site) opened directly with the Read tool, no browser. Every row below is cited to a filename and a position in that file; where a still cannot show something (motion, hover, load timing), that limit is stated rather than guessed past, per K0's own repeated caution that its pre-screenshot scores were provisional pending exactly this capture. Dimension order and columns follow `CONCEPTS-BRIEF.md` section 1. GT verdicts cite the register id, decision id, or K-cluster principle that governs, per `16-g05-decision-record.md` and the K1/K3/K4/K6/K7/K9/K12 "how it applies to GT" sections.

Crimson Global Academy is this batch's one online-school peer and is treated at the length the brief asks for: its legitimacy mechanism for an online offer is examined dimension by dimension, not asserted once and left.
