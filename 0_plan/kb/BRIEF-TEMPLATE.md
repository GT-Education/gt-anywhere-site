# Design Director brief, template (one per mock; `17-design-studio-runbook.md` section 5)

Filled from the knowledge base after G-A and the concept catalog after G-C. No section may be left generic: every line names a source cluster (K0 to K14), an exemplar in `EXEMPLARS.md`, or a register row.

| Section | What to write | Draws on |
|---|---|---|
| Mock id and name | `M01` to `M20`, the working title, the lens order | `05` section 3 |
| Thesis | One sentence: what this direction argues about GT School and why a parent would believe it | `15`, `16`, K7 |
| Exemplars | Three reference pages, each with the one move borrowed and the screenshot filename beside it | `EXEMPLARS.md`, K0, K5 |
| Composition | Grid (columns, gutter, margin per breakpoint), rhythm (the spacing multiples), measure, first-screen structure at 390 by 844, 1280 by 720 and 1440 by 900, how sections differ in weight | K1, K10 |
| Typography | The clamp scale steps used, pairings, where Literata carries and where Inter Tight and Inconsolata serve, the h1 fold arithmetic at 390 and 1280 by 720 | K2 |
| Color and surface | Surfaces per section, where the accent may appear (never as small text), dark surfaces and their text colors, focus ring per surface | K3 |
| Imagery | Photographs by filename, crops per size, treatments, the fallback if a photograph is rejected | K9, `mockups/shared/ASSETS.md` |
| Motion | The moves from the K4 vocabulary this direction uses, each with trigger, duration, easing and reduced-motion behavior; or "still, because…" | K4, D-031 |
| Copy | The fixed slots from `copy.md`; any new wayfinding words recorded in `copy.md` first | `copy.md`, K14 |
| Conversion | Where the doors sit, how the compare anchor lands inside the first two screens, how proof is footnoted | K6, DD rows |
| Acceptance | The rubric rows this mock must reach 4 or 5 on, and the viewport matrix it must pass | `17` sections 4 and 6 |
