## Findings

- **Looks like stock photography, not a real GT photo:** `anywhere-student-home.webp` (girl posed with a robotics kit on an isolated white studio background) and `georgetown-kid-writing.webp` (boy posed with toy trucks as props, also isolated white background, smiling straight at camera) both break from the candid documentary style of every other photo in the folder and should be treated as "avoid" — they read as staged/product photography, not classroom moments, which conflicts with Nari's no-stock-photography rule.
- **Baked-in text or third-party branding:** `georgetown-crowd.webp` has a marketing banner ("Unleash your child's genius at [GT] School") and a QR code baked into the frame — treat any legible copy from it as needing a claims-register check before reuse, not as free-standing background art. `anywhere-slider-2.webp`/`anywhere-slider-3.webp` show a third-party "baduk.club" logo on a board-game backdrop.
- **Repeated/overlapping coverage (same event or session, different crops):** (1) `anywhere-georgetown-visit.webp` and `georgetown-campus.webp` are the same ribbon-cutting ceremony; (2) `anywhere-intensives-2.webp`, `anywhere-section.webp`, `georgetown-3504.webp`, and `georgetown-presenting.webp` all show the same standing desk, laptop, and overlapping cast of kids from one classroom session; (3) `anywhere-academics-left.webp` and `georgetown-support.webp` show the same adult/child pair and setting. Using more than one image from any of these clusters in the same mock will look repetitive.
- **Faces, re-checked 2026-09-11:** no file in the library is free of identifiable children; the three that v1 marked face-not-visible (`anywhere-slider-2.webp`, `georgetown-dscf1931.webp`, `georgetown-guide.webp`) were re-viewed and corrected above. Every photograph is permission-gated under AD-11 and RF-25 before it appears on a new surface.
- **Low-resolution originals:** `georgetown-3504.webp` (540×360), `georgetown-3915.webp` (600×360), and `georgetown-writing.webp` (608×360) are all well under 1000 px on the long edge — fine for a small card or thumbnail, too soft to use as a hero or full-bleed image.
- **Strongest hero candidates:** `georgetown-science.webp` (1440×1208, sharp, clear faces, unmistakable hands-on-science setting), `anywhere-austin-intensives.webp` (1080×1163, sharp single-subject focus, distinctive podcast-studio setting), and `georgetown-crowd.webp` (1440×960, largest file, strong wide proof-of-community shot) are the three strongest candidates for a large hero or full-width placement — each is sharp, clearly in focus, and reads well at large size.
