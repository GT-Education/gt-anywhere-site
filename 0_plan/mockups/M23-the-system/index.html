Two ways to attend

Admissions

Students at or above the 90th percentile. Begin online, then an assessment and a family conversation.

The program

One school, one admissions bar, one mastery-based program. Students advance when they show mastery, not when the calendar says so.

Guides, not lecturers: adults who coach, check in every week and hold the bar.
