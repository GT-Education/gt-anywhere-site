GT School is a selective school for gifted and talented children, in person at our Georgetown, Texas campus or online from anywhere.

The gifted school in the Alpha School network.

Two ways to attend

  • In person Georgetown Campus Explore the Georgetown campus → Two hours of mastery academics each morning on our Georgetown, Texas campus, afternoons for workshops and building.
  • Online GT Anywhere Explore GT Anywhere → Live mastery academics with Guides from wherever you live, and four in-person weekends a year in Georgetown.

Not sure which is right for your family? Compare the two.
