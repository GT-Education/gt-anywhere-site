Proof

What is true of both schools

  1. Admission at the 90th percentile and above.
  2. Mastery-based: students advance on demonstrated mastery, measured with MAP assessments.
  3. Guides, not lecturers: adults who coach, check in every week and hold the bar.
  4. Kindergarten to 8th grade, in person or online.
