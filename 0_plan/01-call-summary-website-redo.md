## Gist

Nari wants a whole redo of the GT web presence, not more point fixes. Phase 0 (removing
wrong content) is done. The immediate deliverable is one landing page that makes the two
schools, Georgetown (in person) and GT Anywhere (virtual), read as two versions of one
school under the GT School umbrella, so parents stop landing on and applying to the wrong
one. After that, the two sites should share an architecture and carry real, maintained
content. Johnny agreed to lead it, said design (not build) is the limiting skill, and
reframed the work around four visitor journeys. Fallback if design exceeds capacity: hand
a mapped structure to a designer.
