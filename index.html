Frequently Asked Questions

Get in touch
Get in touch
  • Is GT Anywhere a full-time school?

    Yes. We are fully accredited by Cognia and TEFA-eligible.

  • Is GT Anywhere right for my child's age?

    GT Anywhere is engineered for students in K-8. This is the critical window where students have the foundational skills to accelerate rapidly, providing an immediate runway to join a statewide tribe of fellow builders and creators.

  • Can my child still participate in Classical Conversations / co-ops / sports?

    Yes. GT Anywhere requires 2 hours daily + 2 hours weekly for live sessions. This leaves ample time for other commitments. Many families use GT Anywhere as their academic core while maintaining other activities.

  • What makes GT Anywhere different from other virtual school programs?

    (1) Adaptive learning platform. (2) Dedicated Academic Advisor focused on your child's long-term trajectory. (3) Continuous virtual cohort of intellectual peers (your child's "tribe"). (4) Physical quarterly intensives with research-grade labs. (5) A focus on Building and Creation, not just consumption.

  • What if we don't live near Austin?

    GT Anywhere is open to families nationwide. The core program is delivered virtually. (Optional, but encouraged) quarterly intensives take place in Georgetown, TX (Austin area) for 2-day weekends, 4 times per year.

  • How much time does GT Anywhere require daily?

    Core academics via TimeBack require 2 hours of focused daily work. Time commitments include weekly (K-5)/bi-weekly (6-8) meetings with your Academic Advisor, weekly virtual cohort sessions, and 2-3 hours of live weekly workshops. GT Anywhere is designed to fit within your existing schedule.

  • Can we use TEFA funds for GT Anywhere?

    Yes. GT Anywhere is a TEFA-approved school, so eligible Texas families can use their TEFA funds for GT Anywhere. For eligibility and how to apply, visit educationfreedom.texas.gov.

  • What is TEFA?

    TEFA (Texas Education Freedom Account) is a Texas state program that gives eligible families funds for private education expenses. Learn more at educationfreedom.texas.gov.
