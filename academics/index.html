Frequently Asked Questions

Get in touch
Get in touch
  • Do you replace teachers with AI?

    No. AI delivers the instruction and calibration (TimeBack/adaptive learning), and humans do the mentorship. Advisors coach, debug, and keep standards high as intellectual mentors.

  • How do you address social isolation in virtual format?

    We don’t run GT Anywhere as a solo online school. Students are placed in daily 12–15 student cohorts and come together every quarter for 48-hour Georgetown Intensives, built explicitly so virtual learning doesn’t mean isolation.

  • Can we use the Texas ESA?

    Yes. GT Anywhere is a TEFA-approved school, so eligible families can use their Texas Education Freedom Account (TEFA) funds.

  • Do you offer K-2?

    Yes, through our Accelerated Foundations track, offering 45-minute sessions for gifted early learners.

  • Is it just more screen time?

    No. Morning work is Active Mastery, and afternoons are Active Creation – building, debating, and sketching. Screen time is used exclusively as a tool for production, not consumption.

  • What if my child doesn't hit their daily mastery target?

    An Academic Advisor steps in to help debug the roadblock live. We treat these as learning opportunities, not failures.

  • How do you prevent academic "rushing"?

    Our Timeback platform requires 90% mastery, which is a high bar for ineffective shortcuts. You cannot move forward until you prove you know the current concept. There is no failing forward.

  • Is GT Anywhere a full-time school?

    Yes. We are fully accredited by Cognia and TEFA-eligible.

  • How do I know if this is a good fit for my child?

    You likely have a GT student if they are bored by slow pacing and frustrated by repetition or want more challenges, not less. Ask your child, “What would make school better?” If they reply: “more learning,” “harder work,” or “more academics,” they will fit in perfectly. We enroll gifted learners who light up around big ideas, hard problems, and measurable progress.

  • Who is the ideal candidate for GT Anywhere?

    GT Anywhere is designed for students in the 90th+ percentile who are intellectually hungry – the kind of learner who wants to go deeper, move faster, and be surrounded by peers who feel the same.
